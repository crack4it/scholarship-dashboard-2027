Frequently Addressed Objections

Clear answers regarding degree equivalence, funding guarantees, and attestation bureaucracy.

Are these programs really 100% tuition-free, or are there hidden fees? ▼
Every program in the database is verified against statutory university law. In Germany, public universities charge €0 tuition (only a ~€150–€350 semester ticket covering public transit). In Italy, regional DSU grants waive 100% of tuition and provide up to €8,000/year in living stipends based on family income. In the UK and China, we only list programs with verified full fellowships (e.g. Felix Scholarships or PKU Yenching).
Do I need an HEC or embassy attestation before I can apply? ▼
No. Almost all foreign universities (UK, Germany, Italy, Netherlands) evaluate admissions based on clean, university-issued PDF scans of your official transcripts. Formal HEC attestation, MOFA legalization, and CIMEA or APS certificates are post-admission requirements needed for enrollment and visa filing between May and August.
Can I apply if my degree is a 2-year Associate Degree (ADA) or in-progress? ▼
If you hold a 16-year equivalent Bachelor’s (such as a 4-year BS or BA in Islamic Studies), you meet standard European entry requirements. A 2-year ADA serves as supplementary credit proof for interdisciplinary subjects (e.g. Politics, Philosophy, Economics). UK and Italian universities accept conditional applications where coursework is completed and the final transcript is marked as in-transit.
What is an "Early Funding Trap"? ▼
Many universities keep their academic admission portal open until May or July, but their scholarship cutoff closes in November or January. If an applicant submits in March, they may get admitted but will have permanently missed the deadline to receive tuition waivers or living stipends. Our platform tracks dual deadlines to protect you from this trap.
Does this platform require any signup, tracking, or subscription fee? ▼
Zero. There are no user accounts, no tracking cookies, and no paywalls. The platform runs entirely in your web browser. You can even download the single-file standalone version (`Scholarship_Dashboard_Standalone.html`) and run it completely offline on your computer.
