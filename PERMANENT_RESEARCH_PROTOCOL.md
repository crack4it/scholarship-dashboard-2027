## 4. The 5-Dimensional Program Evaluation Matrix
Every discovered program must be audited against these 5 standardized dimensions:

### Dimension 1: The 4-Stage Language Barrier & Testing Framework
* **Stage 0 (100% English Medium):** IELTS 7.5 Academic satisfies 100% of admission requirements.
* **Stage 1 (Primary Source Advantage):** Arabic or Persian primary source reading literacy is required or valued (giving the candidate an unfair competitive advantage over Western peers).
* **Stage 2 (University's Own Internal Test):** University offers an on-campus placement test, internal language exam (e.g., on-campus DSH), or conditional admission without requiring expensive external commercial certificates upfront.
* **Stage 3 (Deferrable Language Proof):** Non-English language proof (e.g., German B1/B2/C1) is required only **at enrollment / matriculation** (e.g., April 2027) or by graduation, NOT on the day the application portal closes.
* **Stage 4 (Hard Upfront Gatekeeper):** Advanced foreign language certificate required *at the time of application* (only disqualified if non-English/non-Urdu/non-Arabic/non-Persian and cannot be satisfied).

### Dimension 2: Admission Quotas & Legal Modality
* **Open Admission (*Zulassungsfrei* / *Accesso Libero*):** No legal seat caps. Meeting baseline eligibility = guaranteed offer letter (>95% probability).
* **Departmental Selection / Interview:** Admission based on Statement of Purpose, academic writing sample, and faculty review.
* **Restricted Quota (*Numerus Clausus* / *NC* / Non-EU Cap):** Strict competitive legal limit (e.g., 5%–8% quota for non-EU students). High competition; tagged as "Reach".

### Dimension 3: Cash-to-Departure (CTD) & Economic Reality
Always differentiate between **University Tuition** and **Visa Financial Requirements**:
* **Level 0 (Zero Out-of-Pocket / Fully Funded):** Full government or bilateral stipend covering tuition, living, flights, and visa (e.g., Chevening, Commonwealth, CSC Type A, Stipendium Hungaricum).
* **Level 1 (Low Cash Barrier / Need-Based Living Stipend):** €0 tuition + regional income-based living grant upon arrival (e.g., Italy DSU / ER.GO).
* **Level 2 (Moderate State Charge):** Specialized international tuition surcharge (e.g., Baden-Württemberg €1,500/semester or TUM surcharge).
* **Level 3 (High Liquid Cash Barrier):** €0 public tuition, BUT embassy requires a living expense guarantee / Blocked Account (*Sperrkonto* ~€11,904 in Germany) for visa issuance.

### Dimension 4: Application Friction Index (AFI)
* **Friction 1 (Frictionless):** Direct university online portal, 100% PDF digital upload, €0 application fee.
* **Friction 2 (Moderate):** Central agency portal (e.g., Uni-Assist) or nominal processing fee ($30–$75), digital verification.
* **Friction 3 (High Friction):** Physical certified paper copies required by post, consular legalizations, or preliminary paper evaluation certificates taking >6 weeks.

### Dimension 5: Advantage Calibration (Core vs. Interdisciplinary)
* 🟣 **Core Unfair Advantage:** Programs in Semitic Philology, Islamic History/Thought, Classical Languages, or Intercultural Philosophy where the candidate's combination of **Classical Arabic/Persian literacy + Western PPE frameworks + IELTS 7.5** puts them in the top 1% of global applicants.
* 🔵 **Interdisciplinary & Adjacent:** Programs in American Studies, Empirical English, Cognitive Humanities, Global History, Philosophy, or STS where the candidate's broad social science and humanities background provides strong analytical versatility.
